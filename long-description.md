# HSK4Mastery — App Long Description

HSK4Mastery is an offline Chinese vocabulary learning app designed for HSK Levels 1–4. It provides a focused, distraction-free environment for building, practicing, and reviewing essential Mandarin words and phrases. The app is fully self-contained: all vocabulary, audio, and learning features are bundled within the app, so you can use it anywhere, even in airplane mode. No account, sign-in, or internet connection is required.

## Core Features

### 1. Complete HSK 1–4 Vocabulary, Offline
- The app includes 1,200 words: 150 for HSK 1, 150 for HSK 2, 300 for HSK 3, and 600 for HSK 4.
- All vocabulary is stored locally in a private on-device database, seeded from bundled JSON files on first launch.
- Each word entry includes hanzi (Chinese characters), pinyin, and English meaning. Many words also include example sentences and audio.

### 2. Intuitive Navigation and Layout
- Five main sections: Vocab, Review, Practice, Listen, and Progress, accessible via a bottom navigation bar.
- Consistent top bars and dropdowns for level selection.
- Clean, light-themed interface with clear typography and color-coded comfort levels.

### 3. Vocabulary List and Detail
- Browse words by HSK level, with instant switching between levels.
- Each word card shows hanzi, pinyin, meaning, and your comfort level (Unfamiliar, Getting There, Confident).
- Tap a word to see detailed information: character breakdown, example sentences, audio playback, and a handwriting practice canvas.
- Mark your comfort level for each word, which feeds into your progress and review scheduling.

### 4. Spaced Repetition Review
- Built-in spaced repetition system (SRS) schedules words for review based on your answers.
- Review sessions present cards in three modes: Character, Meaning, and Listening.
- Mark each card as Wrong, Correct, or Mastered to update your learning schedule.
- Struggling Words and Due for Review decks help you focus on high-priority items.

### 5. Practice Sentence Construction
- Two practice modes: Translation Challenge (build a sentence from a translation clue) and Pure Scramble (reorder tokens without a clue).
- Tap tokens to build your answer, check correctness, reset, or fetch a new puzzle.
- Practice is level-specific and adapts to your comfort with words.

### 6. Listening Practice
- Create custom playlists by HSK level and word range, or use presets (First 50, Next 50, Random 20).
- Audio for words and sentences is played directly from bundled assets—no streaming required.
- Adjustable playback speed (0.5x, 0.75x, 1.0x) for flexible listening practice.

### 7. Progress Tracking
- Visualize your progress for each HSK level: see how many words you’ve marked as Confident.
- Overall stats show counts for Mastered, Reviewed, Learning, and Unseen words.
- All progress is stored locally and never leaves your device.

## How It Works

- On first launch, the app seeds its database from packaged files. No network calls are made.
- All features work offline, including audio playback and review scheduling.
- Your learning state (comfort levels, review history) is saved on-device. Clearing app data or uninstalling resets your progress.
- The app does not request internet, network, or sensitive permissions.
- No analytics, ads, or third-party SDKs are included.

## Who It’s For

- Learners preparing for HSK 1–4 exams or building foundational Mandarin vocabulary.
- Anyone who wants a private, offline study tool with no distractions or data collection.
- Users who value transparency: the app’s privacy policy is simple—no data is collected, stored, or shared.

## What HSK4Mastery Does Not Do

- No user accounts, cloud sync, or multi-device state.
- No advertisements, analytics, or social features.
- No online dictionary lookups or streaming audio.
- No automatic handwriting recognition (the writing canvas is for freehand practice only).
- No vocabulary beyond HSK 4.

## Getting Started

1. Install the app and open it. The vocabulary database is seeded automatically.
2. Browse words by level, tap for details, and mark your comfort.
3. Use Practice to build sentences, Review to reinforce memory, and Listen for audio-based study.
4. Track your progress in the Progress tab.

## Summary

HSK4Mastery is a focused, offline-first app for mastering HSK 1–4 vocabulary. It’s designed for privacy, reliability, and effective study—no accounts, no ads, no distractions. All learning happens on your device, at your pace, wherever you are.
